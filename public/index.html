SUPPORT OPTIONS

Choose the support that feels right

If a sound felt uncomfortable, you can stop or skip it. BLOOM does not monitor your answers or alert your care team.

You can contact your care team. If you are in Finland and need urgent help or are in immediate danger, call 112.

For difficult situations, MIELI offers scheduled English-language phone or video counselling. Check its current support and booking information at mieli.fi.

If you are elsewhere, contact your local emergency service or care team. This page is not an emergency service.

ABOUT BLOOM

A field guide, not a diagnosis

BLOOM helps you explore short nature-inspired experiences and keep track of what you report as supportive. The care team preview uses fictional data. BLOOM does not measure brain activity, diagnose depression, detect relapse, or replace your care team.

Your field notes and optional cues are stored in this browser. Community posts are stored in a shared database and can become public after human approval. Use fictional information in a public demo.
